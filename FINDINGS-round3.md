| N26 | Medium (document / verify) | Migration rolls every node | Once N25 is sidestepped (legacy CR stored with `dashboards.replicas: 1`), the migration itself works: the `opensearch.org` twin is created with status, certificate secrets are re-owned, and within seconds the 3.x reconciler logs `Deleting statefulset while orphaning pods`, `Creating bootstrap pod` (for an initialized 3-node cluster; the pod is deleted again a few seconds later), `Starting securityconfig update job`, then `Starting global candidate rolling restart`: all three nodes are replaced one at a time (2m apart), health stayed green, `RollingRestart: Finished` ~5 min after the operator upgrade.
